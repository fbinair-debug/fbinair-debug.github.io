reading-os Privacy Policy

reading-os accesses Google Drive data only for the account owner who authorizes it, solely to list, read and sync that owner's own files. No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's own environments. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
